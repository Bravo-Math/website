Essential Questions

  1. How are fractions, decimals, and percents (FDP) related? What are the pros and cons of each? When should we use each one?
  2. How are FDP used in the real world?
  3. How do digits to the right of a decimal point extend the base 10 number system?
  4. How is arithmetic with decimal point numbers similar to arithmetic with just integers? How is it different?
