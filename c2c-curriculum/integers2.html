Whole Course Resources

In this course, students will master addition and subtraction of whole numbers within 100, from meaning to mental math, from pictures and manipulatives to equations and word problems. Confidence should soar and a very strong foundation will be laid for multiplication, division, and all future math.

Essential Questions

  1. How do addition and subtraction improve upon counting?
  2. How do addition and subtraction help us solve problems?
  3. How are addition and subtraction related?
  4. How can we use concrete objects, pictures, words, and symbols to represent our thinking? What are the pros and cons of each?
  5. What is the purpose of automatic recall? Which facts (equations) are worth learning to this level of mastery and which are not?

Content Sequence

  • Lessons 1.1–1.3: Learn the basic problems and models of addition and subtraction. Express your thinking using objects, number tracks, bar models, ten frames, base-ten blocks, mathematical language, and equations.
  • Lessons 2.1–2.6: Develop mental addition, including automatic recall of all addition facts within 20 and fluency for all addition within 100.
  • Lessons 3.1–3.3: Relate subtraction to addition while developing automatic recall of subtraction facts within 20 and fluency for subtraction within 100.
  • Lessons 4.1–4.2: Put everything together by solving and representing all types of addition and subtraction problems within 100.
