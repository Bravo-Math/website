Learning Rules

One Rule to Rule Them All

Spend the majority of your time on tasks barely beyond your current level of mastery.

Other Rules

  1. Test yourself frequently.
  2. Make an inventory of the skills you need to know. Make sure you can distinguish similar-looking problems that require completely different solutions.
  3. Elaborate whatever you haven't mastered yet. Communicate, represent, teach, and include concrete examples.
